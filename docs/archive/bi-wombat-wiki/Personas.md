## Persona 1 – Dr. Emily Carter
**Role/Occupation:** Senior Life Scientist (Cell Biology)  
**Age Group:** 30–45  
**Location:** Melbourne, Australia  
**Tech Proficiency:** Medium  

**Background**  
Emily is a life scientist with over 10 years of experience. She is highly skilled in biology but struggles with coding and hardware/software interfaces.  

**Goals and Motivations**
- Successfully design and print biological tissue models.  
- Reduce wasted materials (cells, reagents).  
- Work with simple, reliable workflows.  

**Pain Points and Challenges**
- Complex technical interfaces.  
- Waste of valuable samples on failed prints.  
- Limited time to learn complicated software.  

**Behaviour and Needs**
- Preferred Communication: Email, Slack.  
- Information Sources: Research journals, workshops.  
- Tools: Microsoft Office, ImageJ, Prism.  

**Interaction with the System**  
Uploads 3D models, slices them, simulates prints, and monitors jobs without touching RepRap commands.  

**User Story**  
“As a life scientist, I want to simulate and preview my print job, so that I can avoid wasting cells and reagents.”
